Summary

This article is about, how I made an markdown to html converter for this website, what problems I encountered and what is to come. Additionally I will evaluate the key features of this software and what I learned for my future in software development.


How this started

I started at my holiday around August of 2026. I had the idea to boost my productivity for future projects by making a Blog, where I can speak about them in further detail. I envisioned that this would increase the frequency of additions and changes to my projects and moreover make my english vocabulary and grammar better over time.

There was one big aspect that was very important and that is building all the mayor features of this blogging site myself. I knew that there are projects like wordpress or other templates for blogging websites that can be used in my project, because I am not the only one making something like this. But I really wanted to learn from this and I wanted to accomplish something myself.


So what do we need for a blogging site I asked my inner self: Text styling was the first that came to my mind. What is a blog, without having the ability to style text?

Okay, how can you style text on a website? Oh fuck, there is HTML! But I hate HTML. Who wants to write an blog article with HTML? You need standardiced styling that needs to be defined and remembered, so that every article looks the same stylingwise. After some thinking Markdown popped into my head. I loved Markdown since first using it a few years ago after I started publishing my work on GitHub. But how do I make markdown work on a website?


And that was the question which lead to this.
